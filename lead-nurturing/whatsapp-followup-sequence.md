# WhatsApp Lead Nurturing Sequence

This is a practical example for real estate sales teams. Adapt wording, timing and consent rules to your own market and messaging policy.

## Message 1 — Immediate acknowledgement

**Timing:** immediately after lead capture

Hello {{first_name}}, thanks for your interest in {{project_name}}.

I can help with current availability, prices and payment options. Which is most useful for you first?

1. Price list
2. Payment plan
3. Available units
4. Book a call

## Message 2 — First no-response follow-up

**Timing:** 2–4 hours later

Hi {{first_name}}, just checking that my message reached you.

If you prefer, I can send a short summary here instead of calling.

## Message 3 — Value-based follow-up

**Timing:** next day

Hi {{first_name}}, I can send you the most relevant options based on your budget and preferred location.

If you share those two details, I’ll narrow the list down for you.

## Message 4 — Appointment CTA

**Timing:** after engagement or 1–2 days later

Would you like to schedule a short call or online presentation? I can prepare the current availability before the meeting.

## Message 5 — Long-term nurture handoff

If the lead is not ready to buy, move the contact into a lower-frequency nurture track instead of repeatedly calling.

Recommended fields to retain in CRM:

- Source and campaign
- Language
- Budget range
- Preferred location
- Property type
- Purchase timeline
- Last meaningful interaction
- Next follow-up date
- Consent / communication preference

## Operational principle

WhatsApp should support the sales process, not replace CRM discipline. Every meaningful interaction should update the CRM record and next action.
